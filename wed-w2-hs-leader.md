# High School Study Guide · Week 2 — Grace

**Live in the room.** Have this out in front of you. Read the leader prep doc beforehand if you can — this sheet works without it.

**Sep 23 · Black Rock · 30 min after the walk**

One leader per five students. Six or more, split boy/girl. Five or fewer, stay whole.

**Bring:** printed handouts, pens.

**You talk for about eight of these thirty minutes.** More than that and it's a lecture with drinks.

**The clock.** 4 · 5 · 10 · 4 · 2 · 5. If you're not into the handout by minute ten, you're behind. **Take the time back from section 3, never from 4, 5 or 6.** The prayer at the end is not the overflow buffer.

---

## 1 · Land the room — 4 min

Drinks in hand, phones down, everyone sitting before you start. Don't teach over the ordering.

First study of the term, so there's nothing to check back on. Open cold:

> Who's the one person you most want to be impressed with you? Not like — impressed. What do you do differently around them?

Take every answer. Coach, dad, a girl, a group chat. Don't spiritualize it and don't connect it to anything yet. You're just getting the instinct on the table.

If it's flat, go concrete: *what's something you've done purely so somebody would notice?*

*From week 3 on this slot is always "last week's one practice — what'd you notice?" That question is what makes this discipleship instead of a talk. Never skip it, never rush it.*

---

## 2 · Truth — 5 min, and no longer

Point back at what they just said.

> Everybody in this circle has somebody they perform for. Here's the question: do you do that with God?

Then the frame. Draw it on a napkin:

**What I do for God** → self-righteousness
**What God has done for me** → grace

Every religion on earth picks one of those two. Say the memory verse — 2 Corinthians 5:21 — and name what it describes: the great swap. Your sin to Him, His righteousness to you.

**Ask:** which one feels more natural?

Performance, always. Let them say it instead of you.

Then stop talking. The rest comes out of them.

---

## 3 · Equipping — the talking — 10 min

Read **Ephesians 2:1–13** together, split by verse around the table.

Handout. Question 1 warms up the passage; **questions 2 and 3 are the night** — most of the ten minutes lives there.

**How to run it**

- Ask, then wait. Count to ten. The first silence is where someone decides whether to say the real thing.
- Sunday-school answer? Don't correct it. Ask *"what does that look like on a Tuesday?"*
- Follow one thread deep rather than collecting one answer from everyone.
- Your own answer goes in the middle, not first, and it should cost you something.

**If it stalls,** tell them this. It lands hard with teenagers — don't cite it, just tell it:

> A husband has already decided to bail on a date with his wife for a selfish reason. Before he tells her, he buys her flowers and a gift. She's flattered — right up until she finds out why he did it. Then she's disgusted.
>
> That's what performance is. Giving God the things He actually loves — church, generosity, serving — with the wrong motive underneath.

Then re-ask question 3.

**This is the section you cut from if you're behind.** Drop question 1 and go straight to 2.

---

## 4 · Accountability — 4 min

They've already written about where they run on performance. Don't ask it a third time. Ask what it would cost:

> If there is genuinely nothing left to earn — what would you stop doing this week?

Go around. Everyone answers, including you. One sentence each.

**When someone says something real, the next thing out of your mouth is a question about it, not a comment on it.** *"How long has that been going on?" "Who else knows that?"* That's it. No advice, no verse.

**Watch for:** the kid who turns it into somebody else's problem, and the kid who answers fast and shallow to move past it. *"Say more"* handles both.

---

## 5 · Mission — 2 min

> One name each. Somebody you know who's wearing themselves out trying to be good enough.

No plan attached, no commitment. Say the name. We pray for them in a minute and we'll ask about them again next week.

---

## 6 · Supplication — 5 min

Pray for each other **by name**, for the specific thing they just said in section 4. Then the names from mission.

Students pray if they want to. Nobody's called on. If nobody volunteers the first week, you pray for each of them by name — that shows what this is faster than explaining it.

The night ends here. High school does not walk back.

---

## 7 · One practice — as they're standing up

> This week, notice when you do something for God to get Him to like you. Church, being nice, reading your Bible, *not* doing something. You're not trying to stop. Just catch the motive.

Nothing to write, nothing to turn in.

Say it plainly: **next week I open by asking what you noticed.** Then actually do it.

---

## Watch-outs

- "Grace" is a word they'll nod at without hearing. Force the contrast — *what I do* vs. *what He did* — and make them say which one they actually run on.
- Don't rush to Do-or-Done; that's next week. If someone asks "so what do I do?", tonight's answer is *nothing — that's the point.* Let it sit.
- Don't let the walk over eat the session. Leave church on time.
