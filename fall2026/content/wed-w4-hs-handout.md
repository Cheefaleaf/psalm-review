## Five Targets of Prayer

The Lord's Prayer, in order. Use it when you don't know how to start.

**1 · God's honor** — *hallowed be your name.* Who He is. His greatness, His love as a Father. No requests yet.

**2 · God's kingdom** — *your kingdom come.* His will in what you just read, in your week, in the people on your list.

**3 · God's provision** — *give us this day our daily bread.* What you actually need. Contentment with what you're given.

**4 · God's forgiveness** — *forgive us our debts.* Be specific. Then rejoice that it's already promised.

**5 · God's power** — *deliver us from the evil one.* The thing you keep walking back into. Ask for help by name.
