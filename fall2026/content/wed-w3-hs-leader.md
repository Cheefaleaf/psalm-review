# High School Study Guide · Week 3 — Do or Done

**Live in the room.** Read the leader prep doc beforehand if you can; this sheet works without it.

**Sep 30 · Black Rock · ~30 min after the walk**

One leader per five students. Six or more, split boy/girl.

**4 · 5 · 10 · 4 · 2 · 5.** Take overruns from section 3, never from 4, 5 or 6.

---

## 1 · Land the room — 5 min

**Do not shortcut this.**

> Last week I asked you to notice when you do something for God to get Him to like you. What'd you notice?

Everyone answers, including you.

- *"I didn't really think about it."* Ask what their week looked like and walk them into one moment.
- *"All the time."* Ask for one specific instance.
- *"I don't think I do that."* Ask about the moment right before church, or right after something they're not proud of.

Refer back to their examples in section 3.

---

## 2 · Truth — 5 min, and no longer

Two words on a napkin: **DO** and **DONE**.

Every religion in the world spells the way to God *D-O* — here's the list, get it done. Christianity spells it *D-O-N-E*.

Then the objection, before a student raises it:

> Paul says we're saved by faith, not works. James says faith without works is dead. So which is it?

One pass: Paul means **declared** righteousness — Christ's record, credited to you. James means **demonstrated** righteousness — what shows up in someone who actually got it. Declared comes first and causes the second. Reverse them and you're back to Do.

Stop there. The three beliefs come out of the handout, not you.

---

## 3 · Equipping — the talking — 10 min

Read **Ephesians 2:1–13** together, split by verse. They read it last week; this time they're hunting.

Q1 sorts the passage into **we lost it all / He did it all / we get it all**. Do that one together, out loud. **Q2 and Q3 are the night.**

- Ask, then wait. Count to ten.
- Tie back to the opener. *"You said the thing about before church — which of the three is that?"*
- Follow one thread deep rather than one answer from everyone.
- Your own answer goes in the middle, and it should cost you something.

Most people don't reject any of the three — they shave one. Lost *most* of it. He did *most* of it. We get *most* of it. Whichever they shave is where their performance lives. Say that once and let them find theirs.

---

## 4 · Accountability — the honest turn — 5 min

The week's questions:

> Which of the three do you actually have trouble believing about yourself?

And if the room can take it:

> Anyone been running the other direction — using "it's all grace" as cover for something?

Ask the second one evenly, not as an accusation.

Everyone answers. Respond with a question about what they said, not a comment. No advice, no verse.

---

## 5 · Mission — 2 min

> One name each. Same person as last week is fine.

Ask whether anything happened with last week's name. Don't require it.

---

## 6 · Supplication — 5 min

Pray for each other by name, for the belief they said they struggle with. Then the mission names.

Students pray if they want to. Nobody's called on.

---

## 7 · One practice — before they stand up

> This week, catch yourself shaving one of the three. When you feel like you have to make something up to God, ask which one you stopped believing for a second — lost it all, He did it all, or we get it all.

Nothing to write. **Next week I open by asking what you noticed.**

---

## Watch-outs

- "So it doesn't matter what I do" is James's own worry and a good place to be. Answer with a question: what would you do for someone who'd already forgiven you everything?
- The three beliefs are a diagnostic, not a catechism. Don't quiz them.
- Don't preview week 4.
