## The night

1. **Dinner & fellowship — ~30 min.** All three groups together.
2. **Prayer wall — ~10 min.** Everyone, before the split. Every week without exception.
3. **Split.**
   - **Jr high** stays: study ~10 min, then games (trivia, Kahoot, prizes) ~30 min.
   - **High school** walks to Black Rock ~10 min, then small group ~30 min.

The walk lands inside jr high's study block. Both tracks finish together — one pickup.

**Closing prayer is inside the small group.** High school does not walk back.

---

## The two cuts

Same topic, same Journey unit, two depths.

**High school — ~30 min at Black Rock.** Work the unit close to as printed:
- Opener: last week's one practice — what'd you notice? (~5)
- The unit's core idea, briefly (~5)
- Equipping passages and questions from the handout (~15)
- Mission — one name each (~2)
- Pray for each other by name (~3)
- Assign this week's one practice before they leave

**Jr high — ~10 min at church.** One passage, one question, then games. Don't compress the high school session; pick the single thing that matters.

---

## The weekly rhythm

Every high school session ends with **one practice** for the week — nothing on paper, nothing to lose. The next session opens by asking what they noticed. That opener is what makes this discipleship rather than a talk.

Week 4 installs the two tools from Appendix A — **PRAISE** (Pray first · Read · Ask · Interpret · Summarize · Engage) and the **Five Targets of Prayer**. From then on, the one practice usually uses one of them.

---

## Staffing

- **Dinner and prayer wall:** all leaders present. One facilitates the wall.
- **Jr high after the split:** one adult for the study, one or two for games.
- **High school after the split:** one adult walks them over. At Black Rock, **one leader per five students.** Six or more, split boy/girl. Five or fewer, stay whole.
- **Peak need is the small-group block: three to four adults at once.** This is where a missing leader is felt. Young-adult facilitators fill this slot.

---

## What each week gives you

Four documents per week, plus the week's study material:

- **Jr high leader sheet** — run of the ten minutes, the one thing to land.
- **Jr high handout** — one page, finishable in three minutes, theirs to keep.
- **High school leader sheet** — the full thirty at Black Rock, with facilitation notes.
- **High school handout** — the Equipping passage and the questions, used as the discussion guide.

**Teaching is under a third of the high school session by design.** The Journey unit's Equipping, Accountability, Mission and Supplication sections are the point on Wednesday. If you finish having explained the topic well and nobody said anything true about themselves, the night didn't work.
