# CPC Youth — Fall 2026

**Sep 13 – Nov 18 · Ten weeks**

Two tracks running in parallel, one curriculum underneath both.

- **Sunday School — Gather.** Jr high + high school combined, 9:15–10:15, Sep 13 – Nov 15.
- **Wednesday Night — Grow.** Jr high at church, high school at Black Rock, Sep 16 – Nov 18.

Curriculum is **The Journey** (Life on Life Ministries). Journey pages are prep material for leaders — sent in advance each week along with condensed teacher study material. They are not used in class and students never see them.

The **prayer wall** runs whole-group before the split on both days, every week, without exception. It is the connective thread between the two tracks.

---

## The mission

> We gather to faithfully worship God, grow in his grace, and go to the ends of the earth.

The wall version of CPC's mission: *worship God faithfully, grow in the grace of the gospel, and send disciples into the world.*

**Gather, Grow, Go.** Sundays this fall are Gather. Wednesdays are Grow. Spring is Go.
