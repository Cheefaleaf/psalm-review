## Praying — five fingers

**Thumb — who God is.** Tell Him something true about Him.
**Index — what He wants.** His way, in your school, your family, your friends.
**Middle — what I need.** Actual stuff. He's not annoyed by it.
**Ring — sorry.** Be specific. He already knows.
**Pinky — help.** The thing you keep messing up.

**3.** Try it right now. Write one sentence for the thumb — something true about God.
