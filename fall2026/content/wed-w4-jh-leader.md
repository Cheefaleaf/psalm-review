# Jr High Study Guide · Week 4 — Reading & Prayer

**Live in the room.** Read the leader prep doc beforehand if you can; this sheet works without it.

**Oct 7 · Main room · ~10 min, then games**

**The one thing:** you can read the Bible yourself, and here's how to start when you don't know how to pray.

**Bring pens.** Tonight they write.

---

## What you need to know

Three weeks of being told what's true. Tonight they get the two skills to find it themselves — reading and praying without an adult in the room.

Jesus in John 8: *abide in my word* comes first, and knowing the truth follows from it. The habit is the doorway, not the reward.

Jr high doesn't need a method with an acronym. Two questions and five fingers is the whole thing, and they'll still have it in November.

---

## Run of it

**Ask first — 1 min.**

> Who's ever opened a Bible on their own, not at church, and had no idea what to do next?

Say it's normal. Here's the fix.

**Two questions — 3 min.** Read **John 15:1–5** aloud, then a student reads it again.

Then the two questions that are the whole method:

> **What does it say?** and **what's confusing?**

**Spend the time on "what's confusing."** Tell them a question means they were paying attention. Don't answer all of them; let one hang.

**Five Targets — 4 min.** Five fingers, count them off. Have them hold up their hand and go with you.

1. **Who God is** — thumb
2. **What He wants** — index
3. **What I need** — middle
4. **Sorry** — ring
5. **Help** — pinky

Pray it as a group, one finger at a time. You start each. Fifteen seconds each. Nobody has to talk.

**Conclusion — 2 min.** The punchline:

> You just read the Bible on your own and prayed for five things without anyone telling you what to say. That's the whole skill.

Games.

---

## Notes

- Five fingers over an acronym at this age. They'll still have it in November.
- If nobody prays aloud, you pray all five and say listening counts. Don't call on anyone.
- Handout is the keeper. Tell them to stick it somewhere.
- Don't teach PRAISE to jr high. Two questions is enough.
