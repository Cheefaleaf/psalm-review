# Jr High Leader Sheet · Week 4 — Reading & Prayer

**Oct 7 · Main room · ~10 min, then games**

**The one thing:** you can read the Bible yourself, and here's how to start when you don't know how to pray.

**Bring pens.** Tonight they write.

---

## Run of it

**Ask first — 1 min.**

> Who's ever opened a Bible on their own, not at church, and had no idea what to do next?

Hands go up. Say: yeah, that's normal. Here's the fix.

**Two questions — 3 min.** Read **John 15:1–5** aloud, then a student reads it again.

Then the two questions that are the whole method for a twelve-year-old:

> **What does it say?** and **what's confusing?**

Take answers to both. **Spend real time on "what's confusing."** Jr high thinks confusion means they're bad at this. Tell them the opposite — a question means you were actually paying attention. Don't answer all of them; let one hang.

**Five Targets — 4 min.** Five fingers, count them off. Have them hold up their hand and go with you.

1. **Who God is** — thumb
2. **What He wants** — index
3. **What I need** — middle
4. **Sorry** — ring
5. **Help** — pinky

Then pray it as a group, out loud, one finger at a time. You start each one. Fifteen seconds each. Nobody's forced to talk.

**Land it — 2 min.**

> You just read the Bible on your own and prayed for five things without anyone telling you what to say. That's it. That's the whole skill.

Then games. Ten minutes means ten.

---

## Notes

- Five fingers beats an acronym at this age. They'll still have it in November.
- If nobody prays out loud, you pray all five and say plainly that listening counts. Don't call on anyone.
- The handout is the keeper — the five fingers are on it. Tell them to stick it somewhere.
- Don't teach PRAISE to jr high. Two questions is the jr high version and it's enough.
