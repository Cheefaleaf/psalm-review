# Sunday · Week 1 — Glory

**Sep 13 · 9:15–10:15 · Jr high + high school combined**

**Prep:** Journey Blue Year, Gospel Living Unit 1, "Glory — Why We Embrace Christ," pp. 21–32. Read before Sunday. Not used in class.
**Memory verse:** John 1:14
**Readers needed:** 5 — assign as students arrive
**Materials:** both handouts printed · Bibles (ESV) · Holy, Holy, Holy on the screen · Bible Hub open · whiteboard · prayer wall supplies before 9:15

---

## Big idea

Everyone is searching for the one thing that will finally satisfy. The Bible's name for that missing piece is **glory** — not God's majesty, not praising God, but the glory God *gives* to people. We were made to be crowned with it. We traded it away. Now we chase counterfeits. Jesus is where it's found again: *Christ in you, the hope of glory.*

**The mission tie-in:** Journey says what gives glory to God is renouncing counterfeit glories and placing our hope of satisfaction in Him alone. That is our mission statement in one sentence.

---

## Condensed teacher study material

*From Journey pp. 22–25. Read the full pages; this is the spine.*

**The missing piece (p. 24).** Every person — religious or not — is searching for something that will satisfy permanently. Journey's image: everyone is a puzzle with one vital piece missing. People call it happiness, meaning, significance, fulfillment. They look for it in experiences, pleasures, relationships. Most never find it.

**Three senses of "glory" (p. 24).**

1. God's glory — His awesome majesty. *Not this week's sense.*
2. Giving glory to God — acknowledging Him in worship. *Not this week's sense.*
3. **Glory *from* God — the glory He bestows on people to satisfy them.** This one.

Kids will default to 1 or 2. If they walk out with "God is awesome," the lesson didn't land.

**The story of glory (p. 24).**

- *Designed for it.* Psalm 8:5 — God crowned humanity with glory and honor.
- *Forfeited.* Romans 1:21–23 — they knew God, didn't glorify or thank Him, exchanged His glory for images.
- *The search.* Because we weren't built to be without it, we chase counterfeit glories — an addiction to **praise, power, and pleasure**, toy to toy, fix to fix.
- *Recovered.* Colossians 1:27 — Christ in you, the hope of glory.

**"Christ in you" = born again (p. 25).** John 3:3. There's no such thing as a Christian who isn't born again — like being semi-married. Not a real category. The initial deposit of glory ends the search; God keeps pouring more in over a lifetime.

**How to give glory to God (p. 25).** Renounce all counterfeit glories; place your hope of satisfaction in Him alone. Philippians 3:7–9 — Paul counted his gains as loss. Clinging to a counterfeit as necessary for satisfaction is idolatry, even when the thing itself isn't bad.

## 9:25 — Teaching · 25 min

### A · The word — 7 min

**Holy, Holy, Holy on the screen.** Mark every line where we're giving God glory. Give them a minute. Every line qualifies — that's the point.

**Ask:** so what does *glory* mean? Take everything. No correcting yet.

- Expect: God being awesome, praising God, winning, fame, "glory days."
- All in the neighborhood. Hold them.

**Bible Hub, together.**

- Hebrew **kabod** — root meaning *weight, heaviness.* Substance. Something that matters.
- Greek **doxa** — *reputation, splendor, what something is really worth.*
- Quick reference: the Greeks had another word — **kleos**, fame, "what's heard about you." The whole *Iliad* runs on it. Hold that for later.
- Glory = weight. Keep that word in play the rest of the hour.

### B · The songs — 3 min

Second song on the screen. Mark only the lines that are *giving God glory*. Fewer hands. Some songs do it head to toe; some in a line or two.

Before Scripture: giving glory is a specific act, not a mood. So what is the thing being given?

### C · Into the Word — 5 min

**Reader 1 — Psalm 8:5.** Who is being crowned? With what?
Concept: glory is something God *gives to people.* Not only something God has. Made for it.

**Reader 2 — Romans 1:21–23.** What did they know? What did they trade?
Concept: knew God, didn't honor Him, swapped His glory for images. Lost the weight, took something lighter.
Note both directions sit in this one passage — *glorify Him* vs. *the glory of God*. Psalm 8 gave a third: glory *from* God to us. That's today's.

### D · So what — 5 min

Built to carry weight. The weight is gone. Nobody stops wanting it. So everyone gets heavy some other way.

**Ask:** what do people your age trade it for?

- Push past generic. The post, the roster spot, the relationship, the grade, being liked.
- Name the three buckets once answers are up: **praise, power, pleasure.**
- Toy to toy. Fix to fix. It never lands, because it isn't the thing.

**Achilles.** The Greeks idolized kleos. Achilles chose it over a long life — die young at Troy, be remembered forever. Then in the *Odyssey*, Odysseus finds his ghost and tries to comfort him: you're honored like a god down here. Achilles says he'd rather be a farmhand for some nobody than king of the dead. The man who picked glory over everything would trade all of it to be alive as a nobody. Counterfeit glory, at the end, weighs nothing.

Glory isn't a church word. It's the thing under every want you have.

### E · The answer — 3 min

**Reader 3 — Colossians 1:27.** Where is glory now?
Concept: *Christ in you, the hope of glory.* Not hope in general — hope of *that*.

**Reader 4 — John 1:14.** Memory verse. What became visible?
Concept: glory took on flesh. You could look at it.

### F · Back to the wall — 2 min

**Reader 5 — Philippians 3:7–8.** What did Paul count as loss?
Concept: giving glory to God means letting go of the counterfeits and putting the weight on Him alone. That's "faithfully worship God" on the wall.

- **Christ** — the weight is on Him.
- **Community** — we let go of the fakes *together.* We believe something and can say it. We treat each other like it's true.
- **Calling** — what you do because of the first two.

## 10:00 — Breakout · 15 min

High school → foyer with the high school handout. Jr high → stays with the jr high handout. **The two sheets are different.** Check you have the right stack.

Five minutes silent to read and write, then discussion from what they wrote.

**Jr high leader.** Read Colossians 1:15–28 aloud before they start — don't make them find it cold. Open from question 1, which is concrete on purpose. Question 3 is the one that matters; give it the most air.

**High school leader.** Open from question 1 — it is where it goes quiet, so let it. Question 3 is not a test; "Christian, but not sure about born-again" is the most useful answer in the room, so ask what they mean and don't fix it. Protect question 4 for the last few minutes.

## Watch-outs

- Don't let glory stay churchy.
- Nobody needs to recite three C's. Order matters more than memorization.
- Section D is the fullest part. If it runs long, compress Achilles rather than cutting it.
- Twenty-five minutes is the ceiling on the teach.
