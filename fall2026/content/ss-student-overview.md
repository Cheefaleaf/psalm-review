# Sunday School This Fall

**Why We Worship**
Sundays · 9:15–10:15 · Sep 13 – Nov 15

---

## What this is

This fall we're asking one question on Sunday mornings: **why do we worship?** Not just "why go to church," but what all of it actually is — the Lord's Day, baptism, communion, the church — and why any of it matters.

We're using a curriculum called **The Journey**. You won't get homework from it. Everything happens in the room.

---

## How Sunday runs

- **9:15** — Everyone together. Jr high and high school in one room.
- **Teaching** — About thirty minutes. One passage, one idea.
- **Prayer wall** — All of us, every single week. Write one thing. We pray for it.
- **Breakout** — High school heads to the foyer, jr high stays. You get a short handout with a few questions. Five minutes to read and write, then we talk.
- **10:15** — Done. Worship at 10:30.

## Why "Gather"

Our church's mission is to **worship God faithfully, grow in the grace of the gospel, and send disciples into the world.** We say it as **Gather, Grow, Go.** Sundays this fall are Gather. Wednesdays are Grow. Spring is Go.

You're not the junior version of this church. You're doing the same thing everyone else is.
