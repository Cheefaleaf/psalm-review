## Notes

**Sunday week 1 teaches Glory (Blue U1); Wednesday picks up at Grace (Blue U3).** The two tracks run the Gospel Living section in sequence rather than in parallel, which is why Wednesday's first study is week 2 and Faith stays its own week.

**Wednesday weeks 7–9 alternates** are original content, not Journey. If written by late October, swap them in. Each Journey unit listed feeds its alternate.

**Skip all Journey unit assignments** on both tracks — readings, audio, assessments. No homework. On Wednesday the weekly "one practice" replaces them.
