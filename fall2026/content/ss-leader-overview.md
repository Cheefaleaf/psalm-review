## The hour

Hard stop at 10:15 — worship starts at 10:30.

1. **Welcome — 5 min.** Settle, names.
2. **Joint teaching — 30 min.** Whole room, one teacher. Jr high and high school together.
3. **Prayer wall — 10 min.** Everyone, before any split. Every week without exception.
4. **Breakout — 15 min.** High school to the foyer. Jr high stays. Handout questions, then discussion.

If the teach runs long, the breakout is what gets eaten. Watch the clock.

---

## How the Journey unit is used on Sunday

- The unit's **Truth** section (memory verse, In Bible passages, To Summarize) is the 30-minute teach.
- One or two of the unit's **Equipping** or **Accountability** questions become the breakout handout.
- **Accountability, Mission and Supplication are not Sunday.** They belong to Wednesday. Don't try to fit them in.
- Skip all unit assignments (readings, audio, assessments). No homework.

Sunday is the long-study day. The breakout is short and specific, not a second lesson.

---

## Staffing

- **Joint teaching:** one teacher. Others present, no assigned role.
- **Prayer wall:** one leader facilitates.
- **Breakout:** one adult in the foyer with high school, one in the room with jr high. **Two adults minimum. Three is comfortable.**

---

## Breakout handouts

**Two different sheets every week — one for jr high, one for high school.** Same passage, same idea, different questions. Check you're carrying the right stack into the right room.

Jr high questions start concrete and move to the abstract. High school questions start at the idea and push for evidence. Both are one page.

Students take the first five minutes of breakout to read and write, then the leader opens discussion from what they wrote. The handout is the discussion guide — the leader doesn't need separate questions.
