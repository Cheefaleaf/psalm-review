# High School · Week 3 — Do or Done

**Sep 30** · Ephesians 2:1–13

**Memory verse:** *"For by grace you have been saved through faith. And this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God, not a result of works, so that no one may boast."* — Ephesians 2:8–9

Every religion spells the way to God **D-O**. Christianity spells it **D-O-N-E**.

**1.** Go back through Ephesians 2:1–13 and sort it. Write the words or phrases Paul uses for each.

*We lost it all* — ______________________________________________

*He did it all* — ______________________________________________

*We get it all* — ______________________________________________

&nbsp;

**2.** Most people don't throw out one of those three. They shave it — lost *most* of it, He did *most* of it, we get *most* of it. Which one do you shave? What does that look like in a normal week?

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

**3.** Paul says saved by faith, not works. James says faith without works is dead. If you're not earning anything, why be good at all? Answer it in your own words — don't give the Sunday school answer.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

**4.** Galatians 5:1 says Christ set us free. Is there anything you've been doing *for* God that you could stop doing this week and nothing would change between you and Him?

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

---

**This week:** catch yourself shaving one of the three. When you feel like you owe God something, ask which one you stopped believing for a second. We'll ask next week.
