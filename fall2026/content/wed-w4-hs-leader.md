# High School Leader Sheet · Week 4 — Reading & Prayer

**Oct 7 · Black Rock · ~30 min after the walk**

**Bring:** printed handouts, and a pen for every student. Tonight is the one night they write.

**The rule for tonight:** they practice both tools in the room. Explaining PRAISE well and not doing it is the way this night fails.

---

## 1 · Land the room — 5 min

> Last week I asked you to catch yourself shaving one of the three — lost it all, He did it all, we get it all. What'd you notice?

Go around. Tie back to what each of them said last week if you remember it; that they know you remembered is half the point.

---

## 2 · Truth — 4 min, and no longer

Read **John 8:31–32** aloud. Put the chain up:

> abide in my word → truly my disciples → know the truth → the truth sets you free

**Ask:** which one comes first? (*Abiding.*) Everything else in that verse is downstream of a habit.

Then say the honest thing:

> Three weeks in, you've heard what's true. Nobody's given you a way to get at it yourself. That's tonight — two tools, and we're using both before you leave.

Name the obstacle once, because they live in it: most people your age have been taught that truth is whatever's true *for you*. Jesus is claiming there's a truth that exists whether you like it or not, and that it's a person. Don't debate it tonight — just name it.

---

## 3 · PRAISE, practiced — 12 min

Six letters, on the handout. Read them out in twenty seconds — **P**ray first, **R**ead the text, **A**sk questions, **I**nterpret in context, **S**ummarize applications, **E**ngage with God — then stop explaining and do it.

**Work John 14:1–11 together, on the handout, one letter at a time.**

- **Pray first — 30 sec.** You pray, out loud, short. *"God, open our eyes."* Model that it's one sentence, not a performance.
- **Read — 2 min.** Split by verse around the table.
- **Ask — 3 min.** Everyone writes down two questions the passage raises for them. Any question counts, including "why is Thomas like that." Collect a few out loud. **This is the step they'll skip forever if you don't make them do it now** — most people read to finish, not to ask.
- **Interpret — 3 min.** What's going on around this? Jesus is hours from arrest, talking to men about to lose Him. Verse 6 lands differently once you know that. Ask what changes when they know when it was said.
- **Summarize — 2 min.** The handout gives them *"If Jesus is the way, the truth and the life, then…"* three times. That's Journey's own exercise. Let them fill it in silently, then read a few.
- **Engage — 1 min.** Not a step you explain. It's the next section.

**Watch for:** the kid who wants to jump to application immediately. That's the instinct PRAISE is built to slow down. Say so kindly — the point of Ask and Interpret is to stop us from making the passage about us before we've heard it.

---

## 4 · Five Targets, practiced — 6 min

This replaces Accountability and Supplication tonight — the prayer *is* the accountability.

Put the five up: **honor, kingdom, provision, forgiveness, power.** It's the Lord's Prayer as an order of operations, not a new invention. Say that.

**Then pray through all five as a group, out loud, one target at a time.** You call the target; whoever wants to prays; you move on after fifteen or twenty seconds of quiet.

- **Honor** — who God is. No requests yet.
- **Kingdom** — His will in what we just read, and in specific situations. This is where the mission names go.
- **Provision** — what they actually need this week.
- **Forgiveness** — specific, and out loud only if they want. Silent is fine here; say so before you start.
- **Power** — the temptation each of them is walking back into.

Most of them have never prayed in a structure. The structure is what makes it possible to pray for six minutes without running out — that's the thing to point out afterward, in one sentence.

---

## 5 · One practice — before they stand up

> Three days this week. Ten minutes. **John 15:1–11**, using PRAISE, then the Five Targets. The handout has both on it — that's why it's the one to keep.

Three days, not seven. Ten minutes, not thirty. Ask for what they'll actually do.

Say plainly: **next week I open by asking what you noticed** — and next week, that question has teeth for the first time.

---

## Watch-outs

- Do not explain both frameworks and then run out of time to use them. If the clock slips, cut your own talking in section 2, never the practice.
- Silence during the prayer targets is normal and good. Don't fill it.
- Nobody is forced to pray aloud. Say that at the start of section 4, not after someone freezes.
- Collect nothing. The handout goes home — it's the reference card for the rest of the term.
