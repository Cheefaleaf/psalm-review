# High School Study Guide · Week 4 — Personal Worship

**Live in the room.** Read the leader prep document beforehand if you can; this guide works without it.

**Oct 7 · Black Rock · ~30 min after the walk**

**Bring:** printed handouts, and a pen for every student. Tonight is the one night they write.

**Both tools get practiced in the room.** Explaining PRAISE without doing it is how this night fails.

---

## 1 · Land the room — 5 min

> Last week I asked you to catch yourself shaving one of the three — lost it all, He did it all, we get it all. What'd you notice?

Tie back to what each said last week if you remember it.

---

## 2 · Truth — 4 min, and no longer

Read **John 8:31–32** aloud. Put the chain up:

> abide in my word → truly my disciples → know the truth → the truth sets you free

**Ask:** which one comes first? (*Abiding.*) Everything else in that verse is downstream of a habit.

> Three weeks in you've heard what's true. Nobody's given you a way to get at it yourself. That's tonight — two tools, both used before you leave.

Name the obstacle once: most people their age have been taught truth is whatever's true *for you*. Jesus claims a truth that holds whether you like it or not, and that it's a person. Don't debate it tonight.

---

## 3 · PRAISE, practiced — 12 min

Six letters, on the handout. Read them out — **P**ray first, **R**ead the text, **A**sk questions, **I**nterpret in context, **S**ummarize applications, **E**ngage with God. Then stop explaining and do it.

**Work John 14:1–11 together, on the handout, one letter at a time.**

- **Pray first — 30 sec.** You pray, out loud, one sentence. *"God, open our eyes."*
- **Read — 2 min.** Split by verse around the table.
- **Ask — 3 min.** Everyone writes two questions the passage raises. Any question counts, including "why is Thomas like that." Collect a few out loud. **This is the step they'll skip forever if they don't do it once with you watching.**
- **Interpret — 3 min.** Jesus is hours from arrest, talking to men about to lose Him. Ask what changes about verse 6 once they know when it was said.
- **Summarize — 2 min.** The handout gives *"If Jesus is the way, the truth and the life, then…"* three times. Silent, then read a few.
- **Engage — 1 min.** Don't explain it. It's the next section.

**Watch for:** the kid who jumps straight to application. That's the instinct PRAISE slows down — Ask and Interpret stop us making the passage about us before we've heard it.

---

## 4 · Five Targets, practiced — 6 min

This replaces Accountability and Supplication tonight — the prayer *is* the accountability.

Put the five up: **honor, kingdom, provision, forgiveness, power.** The Lord's Prayer as an order of operations. Say that.

**Pray through all five as a group, out loud, one at a time.** You call the target; whoever wants to prays; move on after fifteen or twenty seconds of quiet.

- **Honor** — who God is. No requests yet.
- **Kingdom** — His will in what we just read, and in specific situations. This is where the mission names go.
- **Provision** — what they actually need this week.
- **Forgiveness** — specific, and out loud only if they want. Silent is fine here; say so before you start.
- **Power** — the temptation each of them is walking back into.

Afterward, one sentence: the structure is what let them pray for six minutes without running out.

---

## 5 · One practice — before they stand up

> Three days this week. Ten minutes. **John 15:1–11**, using PRAISE, then the Five Targets. The handout has both on it — that's why it's the one to keep.

Three days, not seven. Ten minutes, not thirty.

**Next week I open by asking what you noticed.**

---

## Watch-outs

- If the clock slips, cut your talking in section 2, never the practice.
- Silence during the prayer targets is fine. Don't fill it.
- Say nobody has to pray aloud at the start of section 4, not after someone freezes.
- Handout goes home. It's the reference card for the rest of the term.
